Additional goals

Add as many extra goals as you want — a new car, additional emergency savings, whatever. These come last in the priority order, so money earmarked for loans, the emergency fund, and the house down payment above is never diverted to them; each additional goal only receives cash that's still left over after the goals before it (including earlier additional goals) are fully funded. Use the ↑ / ↓ buttons to reorder them — the order here sets their priority and goal number in the timeline below.
